## 1. Hostel Accommodation Overview
The campus provides residential facilities across four Boys' Hostels (Aryabhata, Bhaskara, Charaka, Dronacharya) and three Girls' Hostels (Gargi, Maitreyi, Sarojini).

## 2. Allotment Criteria & Priority
1. Outstation students residing beyond a 50 km radius from the campus are given top priority.
2. First-year undergraduate and postgraduate students are guaranteed hostel accommodation on twin/triple sharing basis.
3. Allocation is processed strictly based on the order of application submission and entrance merit rank.

## 3. Application Process & Deadlines
- **Hostel Registration Portal Opens:** July 1, 2026
- **Last Date to Apply for Hostel Allotment:** July 25, 2026
- **Room Allotment List Release:** August 1, 2026
- **Check-in Dates:** August 14 - August 17, 2026

## 4. Hostel Fee Structure (Per Academic Year)
- **Room Rent (Twin Sharing with AC):** INR 65,000 per year
- **Room Rent (Triple Sharing Non-AC):** INR 45,000 per year
- **Mess Charges (Compulsory 3 meals + evening snacks):** INR 42,000 per year
- **Refundable Hostel Security Deposit:** INR 10,000 (one-time payment at check-in)
- Total fees must be paid in two equal semester installments.

## 5. Hostel Rules and Curfew Timings
1. **Curfew Hours:** All students must report to their respective hostels by 9:30 PM on weekdays and 10:30 PM on weekends.
2. **Night Out Pass:** Students requiring an overnight stay outside must submit a digital pass signed by parents at least 24 hours in advance via the Student Portal.
3. **Ragging Zero-Tolerance:** Ragging in any form is strictly banned and constitutes a criminal offense leading to immediate expulsion.
4. **Electrical Appliances:** High-wattage appliances (heaters, induction cooktops, air coolers) are prohibited in rooms.
