## 1. Grading System & Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA)
The institution follows a 10-point Letter Grading System:
- **O (Outstanding):** 90-100 marks (Grade Point: 10.0)
- **A+ (Excellent):** 80-89 marks (Grade Point: 9.0)
- **A (Very Good):** 70-79 marks (Grade Point: 8.0)
- **B+ (Good):** 60-69 marks (Grade Point: 7.0)
- **B (Above Average):** 50-59 marks (Grade Point: 6.0)
- **C (Pass):** 40-49 marks (Grade Point: 5.0)
- **F (Fail):** Below 40 marks (Grade Point: 0.0)

## 2. Attendance Requirement
1. A minimum of 75% attendance in both lectures and laboratory sessions is strictly required to be eligible to appear for the End-Semester Examinations.
2. A condonation of up to 10% (i.e. between 65% and 74%) may be granted by the Dean of Academic Affairs purely on medical grounds with valid documentation.
3. Students below 65% attendance receive a 'Debarred' (DT) status and must repeat the course in the supplementary term.

## 3. Evaluation Pattern & Weightage
- **Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA):** 40% weightage
  - Two Mid-Term Tests (20%)
  - Quizzes and Assignments (10%)
  - Class Participation and Attendance (10%)
- **End-Semester Examination (ESE):** 60% weightage

## 4. Supplementary & Re-evaluation
- Applications for answer script photocopy and re-evaluation must be submitted within 10 days of result publication with a fee of INR 500 per paper.
- Supplementary examinations are conducted annually in July.
