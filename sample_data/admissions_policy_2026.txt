## 1. Undergraduate Admissions Overview
The college offers 4-year Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) programs in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), Mechanical Engineering (ME), and Civil Engineering (CE).

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
1. Candidates must have completed 10+2 (Higher Secondary) examination with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry as compulsory subjects.
2. A minimum aggregate of 60% marks (55% for reserved category applicants) in PCM is mandatory.
3. Qualifying rank in the State/National Engineering Entrance Examination (JEE Main / State CET).

## 3. Key Admission Deadlines
- **Online Application Portal Opens:** June 1, 2026
- **Last Date to Submit Online Application:** July 15, 2026 (5:00 PM IST)
- **First Merit List Announcement:** July 22, 2026
- **Document Verification & Seat Acceptance:** July 23 to July 28, 2026
- **Second Round Counseling:** August 5, 2026
- **Commencement of Classes:** August 18, 2026

## 4. Application Fee
- General & OBC Candidates: INR 1,200 (non-refundable)
- SC/ST/PwD Candidates: INR 600 (non-refundable)
Payment must be completed online via Net Banking, UPI, or Credit/Debit Card.

## 5. Required Documents for Verification
1. 10th and 12th Standard Original Mark Sheets and Pass Certificates.
2. Entrance Examination Rank Card / Score Card.
3. Category/Caste Certificate (if applicable, issued by authorized government authority).
4. Transfer Certificate (TC) and Migration Certificate.
5. Character Certificate from the head of the institution last attended.
6. Four recent passport-size colored photographs.
